The Conference

Two civilizations of order, one committee room

Ancient Egypt ran the longest-lived bureaucracy the world has known — scribes, treaties, and councils that outlasted empires. CLEOMUN borrows that sense of permanence and puts it to work on the problems delegates actually inherit: displacement, disarmament, digital finance, and the politics of a party system. Every committee is chaired to Model UN standard; every delegate leaves with a working knowledge of procedure, negotiation, and the discipline of representing a position that isn't their own.
